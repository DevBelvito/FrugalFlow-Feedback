# Support FrugalFlow

First of all, thank you for using FrugalFlow! 

This app is being built by a solo developer in their free time with a clear mission: to provide a fast, offline-first, and completely private budget tracker without paywalls, ads, or corporate greed.

If you enjoy using the app and want to support its continued development, here are the best ways to do it:

### 1. Report Bugs & Share Ideas (Most Important)
The greatest way you can support FrugalFlow is by helping it grow. 
- If you find a bug, please open an issue in the **Issues** tab so it can be fixed.
- If you have an idea for a new feature or an improvement, let's talk about it in the **Discussions** tab.

### 2. Leave a Review
Taking 30 seconds to leave a 5-star review on the Google Play Store helps the app reach more people who are looking for a privacy-respecting alternative to mainstream finance apps.

### 3. Buy the Developer a Coffee (Optional)
FrugalFlow will always remain free and fully functional. There are no premium subscriptions. However, if the app has helped you manage your finances better and you'd like to support the countless hours of coding that went into it, you can buy me a coffee!

You can use the native **Sponsor** button on the right side of this GitHub repository, or use the "Support Development" section directly inside the Android app. 

Thank you for being part of this journey and for valuing digital privacy!
